Understanding the Rules

Hover over (or tap) the cards to reveal when to use each form.

📅

Plans & Intentions

Which form do we use?

Going to

Use "going to" for actions you have already decided or planned to do before speaking.

"I am going to travel to Japan next year."
👁️

Predictions with Evidence

Which form do we use?

Going to

Use "going to" when there is something in the present that tells you what will happen.

"Watch out! You are going to drop those glasses!"
🤔

General Predictions & Beliefs

Which form do we use?

Will

Use "will" for predictions based on opinions, thoughts, or beliefs (often with think, believe, hope).

"I think robots will clean our houses in the future."
⚡

Sudden Decisions & Offers

Which form do we use?

Will

Use "will" for decisions made at the exact moment of speaking, or when offering help.

"The phone is ringing. I will answer it!"
