🌟 Life Experience

Present Perfect

Use it when talking about things you have done in your life up to now. The exact time does not matter.

Subject + have / has + V3 (Past Participle)
Clue words: ever never before already twice
📅 Finished History

Past Simple

Use it when an action happened at a specific, finished past time. The time period is completely over.

Subject + V2 (Past Simple form)
Clue words: yesterday last week/summer two days ago in 2019
