Traditional Italian Bruschetta

⏱️ Prep Time: 15 mins 🥖 Servings: 4 persons

Bruschetta is a famous appetizer from Italy. Fresh ingredients are needed for this classic dish.

First, ripe red tomatoes are chopped into small cubes, and fresh garlic is crushed carefully.

Next, the tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, and basil leaves are mixed in a large bowl. A little salt and pepper is added for delicious taste.

Then, slices of crusty bread are toasted in the oven until they become golden and crispy.

Finally, the tomato mixture is spread over the warm bread, and the fresh bruschetta is served immediately to guests.

Understanding Present Simple Passive

In cooking recipes, we care about the food and the process, not who is holding the knife. That's why we use the Present Simple Passive.

Subject + am / is / are + Past Participle (V3)
Food item (Subject) Be verb Past Participle (V3) Example sentence
The cheese (Singular) is melted The cheese is melted under the grill.
The potatoes (Plural) are boiled The potatoes are boiled in salted water.
Fresh milk (Uncountable) is poured Milk is poured into the sauce.
💡 Quick Chef Tip:
  • Regular verbs add -ed (mix → mixed, bake → baked, chop → chopped).
  • Irregular verbs change form (cut → cut, spread → spread, shake → shaken).
