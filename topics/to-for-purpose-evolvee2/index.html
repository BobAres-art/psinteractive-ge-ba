Stage 2

The Golden Rule

Master giving reasons in English with this two-part formula.

📐
TO

+ Base Verb

Purpose with an Action

Use to when the reason explains what you are going to do.

  • to have lunch verb
  • to take a shower verb
  • to catch a bus verb
  • to study verb
  • to interview my grandmother verb
FOR

+ Noun / Noun Phrase

Purpose with a Thing or Event

Use for when the reason is a thing, person, place, or event.

  • for lunch noun
  • for a shower noun phrase
  • for the experience noun phrase
  • for a job interview noun phrase
  • for some new clothes noun phrase

Check the difference:

Both express the same real-life goal, but notice the grammar change!

I stopped to have a coffee ☕ vs I stopped for a coffee ☕
