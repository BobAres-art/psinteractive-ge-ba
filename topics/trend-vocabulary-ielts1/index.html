Sentence Architecture: Nouns vs. Verbs

Professional communication relies on alternating between verb + adverb patterns and there is/was + adjective + noun patterns.

Structure 1: Verb Pattern

Subject + Verb + Adverb

• Prices fell sharply last week.

• Sales grew quickly this year.

• Costs have decreased slowly.

Structure 2: Noun Pattern

There was a + Adjective + Noun + in...

• There was a sharp fall in prices last week.

• We saw fast growth in sales this year.

• There was a slow decrease in costs.
