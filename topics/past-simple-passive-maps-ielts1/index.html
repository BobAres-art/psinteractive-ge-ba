Changes Over Time

Designed by Babak Alaee

Part I. The Narrative

Fifty years ago, Greenville was a small, quiet town. In 1980, the city council built a new bridge over the river. They did not destroy the old market, but they relocated it to the square.

Soon after, the old library was demolished, and a new shopping mall was constructed in its place. The local park wasn't reduced; instead, it was expanded to the north. Planners planted hundreds of trees, and a new railway station was opened to the public.

Comprehension

1. Who built the new bridge over the river?

2. Who demolished the old library?

Part II. The Mechanics

When chronicling change, language shifts focus. The Active Voice illuminates the actor—who initiated the change. The Passive Voice elevates the subject of change—what was transformed, allowing the actor to fade into the background.
