How Chocolate is Made

Chocolate is enjoyed all over the world, but the process of making it is quite complex. First, the cocoa pods are grown in tropical climates. When they are ripe, the pods are harvested by hand.

Inside the pods are the beans. The beans are removed and then they are placed in large wooden boxes to ferment. After fermentation, the beans are dried in the sun for several days. Once they are fully dry, they are shipped to chocolate factories around the world.

At the factory, the beans are roasted to develop their flavor. Then, the outer shells are removed, and the inner part is crushed into a thick paste. Sugar and milk are added to this paste. Finally, the liquid chocolate is poured into molds and cooled until it becomes a solid bar.
