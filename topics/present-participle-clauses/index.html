Present Participle Clauses for IELTS Cohesion

Enhancing coherence, lexical flow, and sentence variety in Task 2 Writing

IELTS Task 2 Sample Question: Many cities around the world are facing increasing traffic congestion. What are the causes of this issue, and what measures can be taken to tackle it?
Status: Participles blended into natural essay text

In recent decades, rapid urbanization has dramatically increased vehicular volume across major metropolitan areas. Many commuters rely exclusively on private cars, causing severe road congestion during peak transit hours. Local councils have attempted to widen motorways, hoping to alleviate gridlock without restricting private vehicle ownership.


However, empirical studies demonstrate that simply expanding road capacity induces further travel demand. Private automobiles continually enter city centers, releasing substantial quantities of greenhouse gases and harmful fine particulates. Recognizing the environmental and economic damage caused by such delays, several progressive municipalities have begun investing in electrified light rail systems.


By establishing dedicated transit corridors, cities encourage residents to abandon individual vehicles, creating cleaner and more liveable urban hubs. In conclusion, rather than merely constructing additional highways, urban planners must adopt integrated transport solutions, ensuring sustainable mobility for future generations.

1. What is a Present Participle Clause?

A present participle clause starts with an -ing verb form. When used as an adverbial or cohesive device, it connects two ideas efficiently without relying on repetitive linkers like and therefore, because, or as a result.

2. Common Rhetorical Functions in IELTS Writing

Result / Consequence:
Cities build more roads, and as a result, they encourage more drivers.
Participle Clause:
Cities build more roads, encouraging more drivers.
Reason / Background:
Because governments realize the crisis, they subsidize transit.
Participle Clause:
Realizing the crisis, governments subsidize transit.

3. The Golden Rule: Shared Logical Subject

The implicit subject of the participle clause must match the subject of the main clause. If it does not, you create a confusing "dangling participle" which drops your coherence score.

❌ Driving to work, traffic was heavy.
(Did the traffic drive to work? No.)
✓ Driving to work, commuters encountered heavy traffic.
(Commuters drove and commuters encountered.)

Repetitive & Mechanical Cohesion

Tourism grew quickly on the island. And as a result, local jobs were created. Because hotels noticed the demand, they invested heavily. Therefore, coral reefs suffered damage.

  • Overuses mechanical linkers (Therefore, And as a result).
  • Creates a robotic, repetitive sentence rhythm.

Integrated Participle Cohesion

Tourism grew quickly on the island, creating hundreds of employment opportunities. Noticing this sustained demand, major hotel chains expanded their facilities, ultimately straining local coral ecosystems.

  • Natural cause-and-effect transitions.
  • Elevates lexical flow and grammatical range.
