Present Simple

The Foundation

General Truths & Habits

Employed to articulate steadfast facts, permanent states, or recurrent routines.

Observation The climate forbids labor. She usually spends time indoors.

Present Cont.

The Dynamic Now

Unfolding Actions

Deployed to describe actions happening precisely at the moment of speaking or temporary situations.

Observation Right now, her team is documenting an exchange.

Adverb Position

Frequency Rules

Adverbs of Frequency

always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never

1. Before Main Verb:
She rarely observes...
2. After 'To Be':
He is always punctual.
3. Between Auxiliary & Main:
They are often talking.
