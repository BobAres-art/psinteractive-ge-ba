Riverdale looks completely different today compared to 1980. Forty years ago, it was a quiet industrial center. In 1985, a large textile factory was built in the northern quarter. Many local families moved near the river, and small brick houses were constructed along the southern road. As the town expanded, several ancient trees were cut down to make room for a highway. The historic wooden bridge was destroyed by a heavy flood in 1993, but modern concrete pillars were added soon after to replace it. A decade later, the central open market was transformed into an indoor shopping center, and decorative street lamps were installed along the pedestrian paths. Over time, original cobblestone roads were covered with smooth asphalt.
Subject + was / were + Past Participle (V3)

Use the past simple passive to describe past developments, alterations, or events on a map when the action or the result is more important than who did it.

Singular vs. Plural

Entity Auxiliary Past Participle Example sentence
Singular / Uncountable was built / opened / removed A modern stadium was built in the east.
Plural were planted / built / created Ten trees were planted along the river.

Common Regular & Irregular Map Verbs

Base Form Past Participle (V3) Map Context Usage
build built A library was built near the station.

destroy / demolish demolished The old warehouse was demolished.
replace replaced The roundabout was replaced by traffic lights.
cut down cut down The woodland was cut down.
