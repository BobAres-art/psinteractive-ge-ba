Reading: The Future of Renewable Energy

The transition to renewable energy is one of the most critical challenges of our time. While many countries are investing heavily in solar and wind power, few have completely abandoned fossil fuels. In the past, there was little interest in green technology because it cost too much money. Governments argued that they didn't have plenty of funds to spare for experimental projects.

However, today, a lot of people understand the importance of sustainability. We now have plenty of resources and knowledge to develop better batteries and more efficient turbines. With just a little effort from every community, we can dramatically reduce our global carbon footprint.

Fortunately, there are a few progressive nations leading the way, proving that we don't need much time to make a significant impact if we act decisively right now. We have spent so much time debating the issue, but today many scientists agree that we have lots of viable alternatives to secure our planet's future.

Sentence Types & "Much"

Much is unusual in positive sentences (especially in spoken English). Instead, we use a lot of.

❌ We spent much money.
✅ We spent a lot of money.

Exception: We DO use too much, so much, as much in positive sentences.

"We spent too much money."

Note: many and a lot of can be used in all kinds of sentences.

"Only a..." Limitation

When emphasizing a small amount, we use the word only.

We say only a little (not "only little") and only a few (not "only few").

Uncountable:
"Hurry! We only have a little time."
(some, but not much time)
Plural Countable:
"The village was small. There were only a few houses."
(some, but not many houses)
