The Golden Rule: "If" and "When" stick to the situation

Whether you put the situation first or at the end, the word "if" or "when" stays with the situation. The total meaning does not change!

Step 2: Understand The Structure

Two Ways to Build the Sentence

You can say the situation first or the action first.

1 Situation First

Start with the situation. Put a small comma ( , ) before you say what happens:

[ If / When + Situation ] , [ Action ]

"If I'm late for work, I take a taxi."

2 Action First

Say what happens first, then connect the situation with if or when (no comma needed):

[ Action ] [ if / when + Situation ]

"I take a taxi if I'm late for work."
