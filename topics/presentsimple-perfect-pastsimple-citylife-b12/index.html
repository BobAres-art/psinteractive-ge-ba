Life in Metro City: A Resident's Journal

Read the reflections of Marco, a graphic designer who relocated to Metro City. Look closely at how he discusses general facts, past milestones, and ongoing life experiences.

I live in the centre of Metro City. Every morning, the metro trains run every three minutes, and people rush through the stations with their coffee cups. It is noisy, but it offers an undeniable energy.


I moved here in 2021 from a quiet coastal town. In my first month, I got lost on the underground three times, and I spent two hours trying to find my apartment building. It was stressful, but I quickly learned the layout of the streets.


I have lived here for over five years now, and I have tried more than fifty neighbourhood cafes. The city has transformed rapidly during this time; planners have built several new bike lanes since last spring, making my commute much greener.

Tense Architecture: Present Simple vs. Past Simple vs. Present Perfect

Understanding urban stories requires picking the correct anchor in time. Compare these three core tenses:

Tense Primary Meaning Common Time Cues Formula
Present Simple Daily routines, permanent situations, timetables, and general truths. always usually every day on weekends Subject + Verb(s/es)
The tram departs at 08:00.
Past Simple Completed actions at a clear, finished time in the past. yesterday in 2021 two days ago last year Subject + Verb-ed / Irregular
I bought my bike yesterday.
Present Perfect Unspecified time experience, past actions with present relevance, or duration starting in the past continuing until now. for since already yet ever / never Subject + have/has + Past Participle
I have ridden this tram many times.

Quick Decision Rule

  • Is the time finished (e.g., yesterday, last week, when I was young)? Use Past Simple.
  • Is it a permanent habit or routine (e.g., every day, regularly)? Use Present Simple.
  • Is it an ongoing period (since 2022, for 3 years) or life experience with no fixed date? Use Present Perfect.
