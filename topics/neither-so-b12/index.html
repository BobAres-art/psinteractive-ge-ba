Agreeing with Statements

Master the natural ways to say "me too" and "me neither" using So, Too, Neither, and Either.

🗣️ Real-Life Conversations

Notice how native speakers show empathy, similarity, and agreement in everyday situations without repeating full sentences.

☕ Coffee Shop (Positive: Present Simple)
Alex:
"I really love iced coffee on hot summer days."
Maria:
"So do I! I drink two cups every morning."
Liam:
"Yeah, I do too. It's the best."
Both options mean: "I also love iced coffee."
🎬 After a Movie (Negative: Past Simple)
Chloe:
"I didn't understand the movie's ending at all."
Ben:
"Neither did I. It was way too confusing."
Sam:
"I didn't either. Let's watch an explanation."
Both options mean: "I also didn't understand it."
✈️ Travel Plans (Positive: Present Perfect)
Elena:
"I've visited Japan twice, and I want to go again."
Lucas:
"So have I! Kyoto is magical in autumn."
Nina:
"I have too! Let's plan a group trip."
Both options mean: "I have also visited Japan twice."
🏃 Weekend (Negative: Modal 'Can' & 'Be')
Marcus:
"I can't wake up early on Sundays."
Sophie:
"Neither can I. And I'm not a morning person either!"
Marcus:
"Neither am I!"
Watch out: The auxiliary changes to match the verb (can't → can, 'm not → am).

📚 How the Patterns Work

In English, we agree with someone by matching the polarity (positive/negative) and the auxiliary verb (tense/modal).

✅ Agreeing with POSITIVE

When someone makes an affirmative statement:

  • So + Aux + Subject
    "I love sushi." → So do I.
  • Subject + Aux + too
    "I love sushi." → I do too.

❌ Agreeing with NEGATIVE

When someone makes a negative statement (not, never, can't):

  • Neither + Pos Aux + Subject
    "I don't eat meat." → Neither do I.
  • Subject + Neg Aux + either
    "I don't eat meat." → I don't either.
⚠️ Common Mistakes:
• Never say: "Neither don't I" (Double negative). Use "Neither do I".
• Never say: "I do either" for positive agreement. Use "I do too".

Auxiliary Matching Table

Verb Type Statement Positive (So / Too) Negative (Neither / Either)
Be (am/is/are/was) "I am tired." / "I'm not..." So am I. / I am too. Neither am I. / I'm not either.
Present Simple "She lives here." / "I don't..." So do I. / I do too. Neither do I. / I don't either.
Past Simple "We saw it." / "I didn't..." So did I. / I did too. Neither did I. / I didn't either.
Present Perfect "I have finished." / "I haven't..." So have I. / I have too. Neither have I. / I haven't either.
Modal (can/will) "I can swim." / "I can't..." So can I. / I can too. Neither can I. / I can't either.
