From Factory to Client: The Supply Chain Workflow

Read how EcoBottles Ltd. produces and ships goods. Can you notice how business processes describe what happens to the product rather than who does the action?

Every day, thousands of raw materials are delivered to the central manufacturing plant. First, each batch of recycled plastic is inspected by the quality control department. Impurities are removed during this stage.

Next, the plastic pellets are melted at high temperatures, and bottles are formed using specialized molding machinery. Once cooled, every finished container is checked for leaks. Labels are printed with batch numbers and company branding.

Finally, items are packed into eco-friendly cartons, orders are loaded onto delivery trucks, and invoices are sent automatically to corporate clients. In this system, efficiency is maximized and waste is minimized.

Present Simple Passive in Business

Why use it in production & business?

In business documentation, SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures), and production descriptions, the action and the product are more important than the specific worker doing the task.

Subject + [ am / is / are ] + Past Participle (V3)
Subject Type To Be Past Participle (V3) Business Context Example
Singular / Uncountable is checked / sent / stored The raw material is tested daily.
Plural are packaged / shipped / built Orders are dispatched at 4:00 PM.
Negative is / are not accepted / wasted Faulty items are not sold to retailers.
Question Is / Are ... ? approved / required Is authorization needed for purchase?
The "By" Agent (Optional):

We only mention by + agent when the specific team or person adds vital business value:

"Every delivery is certified by an external auditor."

Step-by-step production flow:
  1. Raw green coffee beans are imported from South America and Africa.
  2. Beans are roasted inside rotating gas ovens at 220°C.
