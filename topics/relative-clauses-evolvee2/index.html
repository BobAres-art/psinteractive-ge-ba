Stage 2: Grammar Blueprint

The Golden Rule Guide

Relative pronouns add extra detail without creating clunky, repetitive sentences.

👤 Talking About PEOPLE 2 Options

Use WHO or THAT

When the extra description tells us about a person or group of people, use who or that.

TRY TOGGLING THE CONNECTOR:
I know the woman who lives next door.
✨ Both options are completely natural and correct!
📦 Talking About THINGS & ANIMALS 2 Options

Use WHICH or THAT

When describing objects, apartments, animals, plants, or ideas, use which or that.

TRY TOGGLING THE CONNECTOR:
I live in an apartment that is near the park.
🌿 Never use "who" for objects or plants!
Secret Superpower

The Magic of "THAT"

Notice how THAT appears on both sides? It acts like a bridge for both people and things!

WHO Only People 👤
THAT ⭐ People & Things 👥📦
WHICH Only Things 🚗🌲
