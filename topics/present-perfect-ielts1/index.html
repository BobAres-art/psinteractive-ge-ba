Core Meaning

The Bridge Between Past & Present

The Present Perfect connects two worlds: the past (when the action occurred or started) and the present moment (where we experience the result or continuing reality).

Past Origin
⚡
"The town council built the bridge"
(Action completed before now)
🔗 Present Perfect Connection
Current Situation
✨
"It has connected the districts ever since"
(Active present result)
Formula: The town council has built a bridge = The construction started in the past, and it directly serves our daily community right now.

Key Distinction: "He's" = "He is" OR "He has"?

Look at what follows the contraction. If followed by a Past Participle (V3), it means has:

"He's finished the project."

➔ Followed by V3 finished = He has finished.

"He's very happy today."

➔ Followed by adjective happy = He is happy.
