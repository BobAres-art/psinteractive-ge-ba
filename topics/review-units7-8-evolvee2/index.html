Stage 2: The Grammar Rules

Purpose: TO vs. FOR

Look closely at the word right after the preposition:

Pattern A Action / Activity

TO + Base Verb

Use TO when your reason is an action or activity. Always follow with the basic dictionary form of the verb.

"I went to the kitchen to get a glass of water."
"She called me to invite me to the party."
"He wears running shoes to run in the park."
⚠️ Common Mistake: Never say "for get" or "for to get". Use to get!
Pattern B Thing / Person / Event

FOR + Noun (Thing)

Use FOR when your reason is a noun (a thing, an item, an event, or a person).

"I went to the kitchen for a glass of water."
"We met at 1:00 PM for lunch."
"Liam bought balloons for the celebration."
💡 Quick Test: Is the next word an action you do? Use TO. Is it an object or event? Use FOR!

Stage 3: Essential Grammar Partner

Verb Patterns with Simple Verbs

When two verbs meet in a sentence, which form comes second?

want • need • enjoy • decide
1

Verbs followed by: TO + Verb

These simple verbs express a wish, plan, or future action:

want + to + verb
"I want to eat some pasta."
need + to + verb
"She needs to sleep early."
decide + to + verb
"We decided to stay home."
hope + to + verb
"They hope to visit soon."
⚠️ Never say: "I want eating" or "I want for eat". Always use want to + verb!
2

Verbs followed by: Verb + -ING

These simple verbs describe hobbies, activities, or general experiences:

enjoy + -ing
"I enjoy cooking dinner."
finish + -ing
"He finished doing his homework."
mind + -ing
"I don't mind waiting."
like / love + -ing
"They love swimming in summer."
⚠️ Never say: "I enjoy to read". Always say I enjoy reading!
Purpose vs. Verb Patterns at a Glance:
Stating Purpose (Action): "I went out to buy milk."
Wish / Need (Verb Pattern): "I want to buy milk."
Enjoyment / Hobby: "I enjoy buying fresh food."

Rule Mastery Summary

Express your reasons with confidence!

• TO + base verb for actions (to study, to meet)
• FOR + noun for items or events (for lunch, for a rest)
• want / need / decide + TO + verb
• enjoy / finish + verb-ING
